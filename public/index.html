Resilient Youth Voices (RYV)

“People United Under One Voice Can Never Be Defeated.”

A youth-led movement in Eswatini dedicated to HIV advocacy, equality, and human rights. We empower the next generation to lead with courage and inclusion.

Join Us Become a Peer Advocate Partner With Us

Our Vision for Eswatini

We envision a world where every young person, regardless of their status, has equal access to rights, healthcare, and leadership opportunities.

Youth Empowerment

Providing tools and skills to help youth take charge of their futures.

HIV Advocacy

Breaking the silence and fighting stigma through education and support.

Human Rights

Standing firm against inequality and protecting the rights of the marginalized.

Community Engagement

Building strong, inclusive networks across all regions of Eswatini.

Voices of the Youth

" whatever you believe, you can achieve - regardless of your background, HIV status, or any stigma and discrimination you may face. remember, every extraordinary person was once just an ordinary person, just like you. never let your HIV status define your future or who you are, your potential is limitless."

— Mthobisi Mdluli, RYV Managing Director (Founder) and Comms Manager

" you're strong, and i believe in you. living with hiv can be tough, but it's not just about the virus; it's about the person you are. you deserve respect, care, and compassion – from others and yourself. your worth isn't defined by your hiv status. you're not alone in this; there are people who care and want to support you. let's focus on what you need to thrive."

— Siphesihle Sthole, RYV HR Manager and Public Relations Executive

"never let stigma or discrimination define who you are. let your courage, love, and strength shine brighter than hate. walk your journey with pride,peak your truth with power, and remember — hiv doesn’t end your dreams, it strengthens your story. let’s break the silence, support one another.."

— Nkosiyenza Mamba, RYV Executive Director and Comms Assistant

" Your disability does not define your value, and society's judgment does not limit your future. Keep standing tall resilience always shines brighter than discrimination."

— Zinhle Msibi, HIV prevention and awareness Ambassador

" Let get test if u don't know your HIV status Let are protect our self from unwanted pregnancy as young mothers"

— Busiswa Dlamini, Young mothers support Coordinator

" whatever negativity comes your way, remember — it can't make you HIV negative, but your strength can make you unstoppable."

— Lunga Vilakati, RYV Director of Logistics and Project Assistant

" living with HIV as a young person doesn't define you, it is how you overcome it that truly shapes your future. be resilient, hopeful, and spread love and awareness amongst each other."

— Simphiwe Vilane, RYV Project Coordinator
