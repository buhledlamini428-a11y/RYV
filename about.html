About RYV

Dedicated to resilience, equality, and the power of youth voices in Eswatini.

Our Story &
Background Information

Resilient Youth Voices (RYV) is a youth-led, non-profit organization in Eswatini that empowers adolescents and young people, especially those living with HIV and marginalized groups, through advocacy, inclusion, and policy influence.

RYV promotes equal rights, fights stigma and discrimination, and creates safe spaces for youth participation, leadership, and community engagement while advancing equality, resilience, and youth empowerment.

What We Stand For

Inclusion & Equality

We believe every youth, regardless of status or background, deserves a seat at the table and a voice in decision-making.

Fighting Stigma

Focused on dismantling the barriers of discrimination against those living with HIV through education and empathy.

Youth Leadership

Empowering the next generation of Eswatini's leaders through mentorship and strategic skill-building.
